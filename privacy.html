Privacy

This page explains, in plain language, how this website handles personal information. It will be updated as the company’s legal and data-protection arrangements are completed.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, topic and message, plus your confirmation that we may use them to reply.

Why

To read and answer your message. We do not use it for anything else.

Where it is stored

Messages are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Only the site owner can read them.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics. The browser may keep a short timestamp locally to limit repeated form submissions. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your message at any time by sending a new message through the contact form.
